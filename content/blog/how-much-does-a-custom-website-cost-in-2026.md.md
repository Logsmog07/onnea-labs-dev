# How Much Does It Really Cost to Build a Custom Website in 2026?

Every time a business owner asks a web agency for a price, they usually get the dreaded answer: *"It depends."* But in 2026, transparency is non-negotiable. While you can technically get a website for $15 a month using a DIY drag-and-drop builder, a *custom-built* website engineered for high performance, generative SEO, and lead generation is a completely different asset. 

## The 2026 Custom Website Cost Breakdown

To give you a realistic idea of what professional web development agencies charge this year, here is a breakdown based on the complexity and scope of the project:

**1. Small Business / Informational**
* **Estimated Cost:** $1,000 – $8,000
* **Best For:** Local services, consulting, brand portfolios

**2. Mid-Level & E-Commerce**
* **Estimated Cost:** $3,000 – $25,000
* **Best For:** Growing D2C brands, online stores, complex CMS needs

**3. Custom Web Apps & Enterprise**
* **Estimated Cost:** $10,000 – $90,000+
* **Best For:** SaaS platforms, multi-vendor marketplaces, complex portals

> **Note:** These are average global agency rates. Your exact cost will fluctuate based on your specific tech stack, API integrations, and the geographical location of your development team.
