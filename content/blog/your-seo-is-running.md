# Your SEO Is Running, But Is It Working For You or Against You?

**Getting traffic is not the same as getting business. If your SEO isn't built around the right keywords, you're spending money to attract people who will never buy from you.**

## Your website is getting traffic. So why aren't you getting leads?

You invested in SEO. Pages are ranking. Traffic is coming in.

But the enquiries feel off. The leads aren't the right fit. And every month, you're paying for a strategy that isn't moving the needle on revenue.

Here's the uncomfortable truth most agencies won't tell you:

**Traffic is not the goal. The right traffic is.**

And if your SEO was built around the wrong keywords, you're not just getting zero return. You're actively paying to attract people who will never become customers.

---

## The silent money drain: wrong keywords

This is the most common and most expensive mistake in B2B SEO. A business picks keywords based on search volume—the bigger the number, the better it looks in a report. So they go after terms like "digital marketing agency" or "business growth solutions."

Think about who actually searches those terms. Students doing research. Competitors checking the market. People in completely different industries with completely different problems. Not your buyer.

Meanwhile, your ideal client—a SaaS founder looking specifically for a B2B content and SEO partner—is typing something much more specific. And your site isn't showing up for that, because all your budget and effort went into keywords that were never going to convert.

Wrong keywords don't just waste money. They actively mislead your strategy because your analytics show traffic, your rankings look decent, and everything appears to be working. Until you look at your pipeline and realise nothing is closing.

---

## Going after high-competition keywords is a fight you can't win

Here's what happens when a B2B business targets highly competitive keywords without the domain authority to back it up.

You're on page four. Page five. Maybe page two on a good day.

The top three results? They're held by companies that have been publishing content for a decade, have thousands of backlinks, and a dedicated SEO team working on those positions every single month.

You are not going to outrank them on that keyword. Not without years of investment and a budget most B2B businesses simply don't have.

And while you're burning time and money fighting for a position that isn't coming, your competitors who targeted smarter, lower-competition keywords are already on page one, picking up the exact buyers you want.

This is not a traffic problem. It's a strategy problem. The fix isn't to work harder on the same keywords. It's to stop targeting the wrong ones entirely.

---

## What dead SEO actually costs you

Let's make this concrete. If your SEO budget is ₹50,000 a month and the traffic it's generating has a 0% conversion rate because it's the wrong audience—you're not spending ₹50,000 on marketing.

You're spending ₹50,000 on noise.

And it compounds. Every month of wrong-keyword targeting is another month where your competitors with smarter strategies are building authority, ranking for the right terms, and closing the clients you should be closing.

Dead SEO has a cost. It's just hidden inside a report that shows traffic going up while your pipeline stays flat.

---

## What smart B2B SEO actually looks like

The businesses that get real results from SEO aren't the ones chasing the biggest keywords. They're the ones who understand exactly who their buyer is, what that buyer types into Google at the moment they're ready to find a solution, and build their entire strategy around those specific, high-intent terms.

That means:

* **Targeting buyer-intent keywords, not volume keywords.** A keyword with 200 monthly searches from people actively looking for your exact service is worth more than a keyword with 20,000 searches from people who have nothing to do with your business.
* **Competing where you can actually win.** Low to mid-competition keywords in your specific niche are where B2B businesses build real organic pipelines, not in the overcrowded lanes where the big players have already parked for years.

## FAQ

### Why is my website getting traffic but no leads?
Usually because the keywords driving that traffic don't match what your actual buyers are searching. High-traffic keywords often attract the wrong audience — researchers, students, or people in completely unrelated industries.

### What are wrong keywords in B2B SEO?
Keywords that are too broad, too competitive, or not aligned with buyer intent. If you're ranking for terms that your ideal client would never actually type when looking for your service — those are the wrong keywords, regardless of their search volume.

### Is competing for high-competition keywords worth it for B2B businesses?
Rarely — especially without significant domain authority and budget. Most B2B businesses see far better results targeting specific, lower-competition keywords where they can realistically rank and where the searchers are closer to making a buying decision.

### How do I know if my SEO is targeting the wrong keywords?
Look at your traffic alongside your conversion rate. If traffic is growing but enquiries aren't — or if the enquiries you're getting aren't the right fit — that's a clear signal your keyword strategy isn't aligned with your actual buyer.

### What's the difference between traffic and the right traffic?
Traffic is anyone landing on your website. The right traffic is people who have the problem you solve, the budget to hire you, and the intent to find a solution. SEO that doesn't bring the right traffic is wasted spend, no matter how the numbers look in a report.
